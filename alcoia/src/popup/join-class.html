Before you join

What your instructor can see

Your instructor sees aggregate results only — things like "most of the class understood section 2." Nothing about your own reading, individually, is ever visible to them.

The one exception: if you choose to submit a reading receipt for a specific assignment, your instructor sees that one receipt. Nothing else, and never automatically.

What your instructor can see

This class reports individual results. Your instructor can see how you did on this class's assignments, by name.

This was set when the class was created and cannot be changed after you join. If you do not want that, do not join this class.

Your classes

You can join another class any time — joining doesn't require leaving one you're already in.
