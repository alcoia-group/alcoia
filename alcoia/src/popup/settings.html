Account-based learning

Let Alcoia remember what you've learned across documents and over time, so reviews and learning activities can fit you better. It is on by default for your account. You can turn it off, export it or delete it here at any time.

This memory exists for your own Alcoia learning experience. It is not used by Alcoia for advertising, sold to third parties, or used to build profiles about you for unrelated purposes.

What is remembered, and who can see it

Remembered: which ideas you have been asked about, how those questions went, when a review is due, and related learning information needed to provide the feature.

Not remembered here: the pages you visit, web addresses, what you type, how you scroll, or a record of everything you read. It remembers learning state, not a surveillance history of your reading.

Who can see it: you can see it, export it and delete it. Other learners cannot see it, and instructors cannot see it as your private learning memory. It is separate from class-level results, which are kept anonymously for each assignment, and some class features may use aggregated learning information under their own privacy rules. Service providers process it on Alcoia's behalf to run the service.

Your notes, highlights and quizzes stay on this device and are separate from this.

Turn account-based learning back on?
  • It is on by default. You can turn it off whenever you like, and Alcoia still works without it.
  • Alcoia will keep a private learning memory in your account across documents and over time: which ideas you were asked about, how they went, and when a review is due.
  • It is kept for your own learning experience, not for advertising, sale or unrelated profiling.
  • It is not a record of the pages you read, and your notes and highlights stay on this device.
  • Turning it off keeps what is stored. You can delete it separately.

Turning this off stops Alcoia from using or adding to your account-based learning memory. It does not delete memory you have already stored.

Download a copy of the private learning memory Alcoia currently stores for you.

Delete learning memory?

This permanently deletes the learning memory Alcoia has stored for your account. It does not delete your Alcoia account, class membership, billing information, or data stored on this device. This cannot be undone. It does not turn account-based learning off.
