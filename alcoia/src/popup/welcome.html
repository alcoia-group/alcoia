alcoia is installed

alcoia only works on pages you choose. It reads nothing on a site until you switch it on.

  1. Open a page you want to read.
  2. Click the alcoia button in your toolbar. If you do not see it, open the puzzle-piece menu and pin alcoia.
  3. Switch on Use alcoia on this site. Your browser asks you to allow that one site.

To use alcoia on every site, open Settings from the alcoia button and switch on Every site.

alcoia never runs on banking, payment, email, health or password-manager pages, or on any page with a password or card-number field. You can add more sites to that list in Settings.
