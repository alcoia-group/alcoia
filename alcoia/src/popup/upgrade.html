What we will never charge for

Dyslexia mode, the focus ruler, read aloud and bionic reading stay free, permanently. They are the features some people need in order to read at all, and putting them behind a plan would be charging for access rather than for help.

Students: 40% off Reader with an academic email address, on request. Regional pricing is available for Africa, South Asia and Latin America — the cost of a year should not be a week's income.

Questions

Do I need a webcam?

No. Detection runs on how you move through the text — pace against difficulty, re-reading, where you stop — and none of that needs a camera or a permission prompt. The webcam is an optional extra that adds only whether you are at the screen, and it is off until you turn it on.

Is my video uploaded?

Never. No frame, image or video ever leaves your machine — with the camera on, everything is processed locally and only aggregates are kept. The text of the passage you are reading is sent to a server to generate a question or an explanation. That is the one thing that leaves your device, and we would rather say so here than let you find out later.

Can my school see my reading?

Only what you choose to submit. Receipts are built on your machine, shown to you in full before they go anywhere, and never sent in the background. There is no mode that lets anyone watch you read without your action, and there will not be one.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep the free plan, and everything saved on your device — notes, highlights, receipts — stays where it is. Nothing is deleted and nothing is held hostage.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Notes, highlights and session reports all export as JSON from their own pages, on every plan including the free one.
